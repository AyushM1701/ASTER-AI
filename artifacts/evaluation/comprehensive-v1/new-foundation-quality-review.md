## Material source holds

- **chunk_ab6445963e541cdcb7496c2b** (audit-osu-anatomy2019-18-6-blood-typing; Antigens, Antibodies, and Transfusion Reactions): Defines antigen as necessarily non-self and necessarily eliciting a defensive response. This conflates antigenicity with immunogenicity and excludes self antigens. Do not emit as a general immunology definition. Policy: **explanation_only**. Evidence: [source 1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/mesh?Cmd=DetailsSearch&Db=mesh&Term=%22Haptens%22%5BMeSH+Terms%5D), [source 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4780685/).
- **chunk_5fed1787bbc6a16e47ffa63b** (audit-osu-anatomy2019-18-6-blood-typing; The ABO Blood Group): Describes ABO A and B antigens as both glycoproteins. Antigen specificity is carbohydrate, present on glycoprotein and glycolipid carriers; bounded ABO presence/absence note can omit this faulty chemical definition. Policy: **explanation_only**. Evidence: [source 1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK2267/).
- **chunk_3820eff8e88c306d9dd6fb25** (audit-osu-anatomy2019-18-6-blood-typing; Rh Blood Groups): Says only Rh antigen D is clinically important. Anti-C, anti-E, anti-c etc are clinically significant. Raw context also includes pregnancy and prophylaxis instructions unsuitable for generic concept fallback. Policy: **exclude**. Evidence: [source 1](https://www.fda.gov/media/189958/download).
- **chunk_6fd4273800528a50d5511bba** (audit-osu-anatomy2019-18-6-blood-typing; Determining ABO Blood Types): ABO/Rh typing instruction says blood should be tested for Rh antibodies; RhD antigen typing using anti-D reagent is distinct from antibody screening. Policy: **explanation_only**. Evidence: [source 1](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/blood-transfusion-safety/guidelines-and-principles-for-safe-blood-transfusion-practice-module-3-.pdf?sfvrsn=e942e4c9_1).
- **chunk_9b237083eb29b1c8324225d1** (audit-osu-anatomy2e-cell-membrane; Active Transport): Mislabels sodium entry through a passive channel driven by an existing gradient as secondary active transport. Secondary active transport couples downhill transport to uphill transport of another substrate; channel flux is passive. The later symporter paragraph does not repair the defining example. Policy: **explanation_only**. Evidence: [source 1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2418734/), [source 2](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26815/).
- **chunk_0d005e8f5ce5ca37acd7410c** (audit-osu-anatomy2e-cell-membrane; Other Forms of Membrane Transport): Transferrin receptor-mediated endocytosis is assigned generally to red blood cells without the required erythroid precursor/reticulocyte qualification. Original experiments distinguish developing reticulocytes from mature RBCs. Also embeds dated CF survival/statistics and dangling external microscope prompt. Policy: **explanation_only**. Evidence: [source 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2112509/), [source 2](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0006497120792608).
- **chunk_7aaebeeb657f4bff80e0d67b** (audit-osu-anatomy2e-cell-membrane; Other Forms of Membrane Transport): Calls chloride movement through CFTR active transport solely because its channel gating involves ATP. CFTR is an ATP-gated ion channel; downhill ionic conduction is distinct from ATP-driven uphill pumping. Avoid this explicit exception-to-passive-transport teaching. Policy: **exclude**. Evidence: [source 1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28340353/), [source 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7512348/), [source 3](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26815/).
- **chunk_a314f42eb0211d6b1b75d915** (audit-osu-anatomy2e-hemostasis; Clotting Factors Involved in Coagulation): Counts vitamin K among the twelve known clotting factors. Vitamin K is a cofactor/coenzyme for gamma-carboxylation of vitamin-K-dependent proteins, rather than a numbered coagulation factor. Begins with dangling Both of these merge after extraction. Policy: **explanation_only**. Evidence: [source 1](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminK-HealthProfessional/), [source 2](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?dcmsLinks=true&ui=D014812).

## Coverage and extraction limitations

Facial muscles retain only one introductory paragraph, ocular muscles omit named muscles and cranial-nerve mapping, mastication omits attachments/innervation, platelet plug retains only two sentences without adhesion/activation/aggregation, and major salivary gland anatomy is missing. Broad cranial-nerve mnemonic material cannot replace nuclei, foramina, branches, examination or lesions. Fungal general morphology does not cover oral Candida, microscopy/culture interpretation or laboratory competency. Tables excluded to avoid extraction errors need authoritative replacements before a complete curricular claim.

Additional review flags below deliberately distinguish uncertainty/oversimplification from the verified material holds. They are not all proven errors. Raw treatment passages, dangling video/link prompts, historical numbers and mixed chapter-summary paragraphs should not be emitted for default concept questions. Human faculty review of current clinical facts and newly written notes remains necessary.
